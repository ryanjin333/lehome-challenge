The approved design is implemented in dependency order:

1. `2026-08-03-groot-flywheel-core-data.md` — pure episode, export, matrix, and hard-mining contracts.
2. `2026-08-03-groot-flywheel-autonomous-rollouts.md` — Isaac recording, snapshots, randomization, campaigns, and capacity measurement.

Plans 1 and 2 establish the statistically useful baseline and automated hard-case queue. Plan 3 converts selected failures into accepted expert demonstrations and recoveries. Plan 4 freezes those labels into the next independently trainable policy revision.

Do not skip the completion gate at the end of a plan. Paid remote acceptance begins only where a plan explicitly names it, and renting, stopping, or destroying infrastructure remains outside these implementation commands.
